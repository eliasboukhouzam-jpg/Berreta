# 03 — Materials atlas: the minerals behind the weapons

> Brainstorm draft, 13 Oct 2026. This file is the site's **second axis**: every material gets its own page, linking every weapon that depended on it.
> Legend: untagged = 🟡 consensus · ✅ hard data · ⚠️ estimate/verify. Market shares are approximate and **must be refreshed from USGS Mineral Commodity Summaries 2026** before publishing.
> "Chokepoint today" = who dominates mining **and/or processing**. Processing is usually the real bottleneck.

Status flags: 🟥 critical / under active export controls · 🟧 concentrated supply · 🟩 diversified · ⬜ historical only

|---|---|---|---|---|---|

| **Tin (Sn)** | Cassiterite | **Bronze**; today solder in every electronic weapon | Central Asia, Cornwall, Iberia, Brittany, Erzgebirge, Malaya, Bolivia | China, Indonesia, Myanmar (Wa State), Peru, DRC | 🟧 |

| **Carbon (charcoal → coke)** | Wood → coal | Smelting fuel for all iron and bronze; gunpowder (charcoal) | Every forest: **deforestation was the first environmental cost of war** | Coking coal (Australia, US, Russia); EAF steel uses scrap | 🟩 |

| **Silver (Ag)** | Argentite, galena | **Financing:** Athens' fleet (Laurion), Habsburg wars (Potosí); Manhattan Project magnet coils ✅; today electronics, brazing | Laurion, Potosí, Zacatecas | Mexico, Peru, China | 🟩 |

| **Saltpeter / nitrates (N)** | Niter (KNO₃), nitratine (NaNO₃) | **Gunpowder (~75%)**, then all explosives & propellants | Nitrous earth (stables, caves), **Bihar (India)**, French nitre beds, **Atacama (Chile)** | **Synthetic since 1913** via Haber-Bosch from **natural gas**. | **Pyrite (FeS₂)** | "Fool's gold" | Wheellock ignition (1500s) | Everywhere | — | ⬜ |

## Material-page template (what each atlas page shows)

1. **Card:** symbol, one-line "why war needs it"
2. **Timeline strip:** first weapon use → today (auto-built from `02-master-timeline.md` links)
3. **Where it comes from:** historical map + today's producer map (USGS data)
4. **From rock to weapon:** ore → concentrate → refined metal → alloy/compound → component → weapon
5. **Chokepoint meter:** share of the top country in mining vs processing (processing is usually the real risk)
6. **Who paid:** miners, environment
7. **Live box:** current export controls & policy (dated, sourced: this box goes stale fastest)
