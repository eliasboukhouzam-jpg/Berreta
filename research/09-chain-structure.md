# 09 — How the chain is told: a brief past, a deep present

> Draft, 13 Oct 2026. Answers the brief: *show the old revolutions briefly, give a sense of the scale of evolution, then go deep on the last ~10 years and the weapons that changed how people think about war.*
> Confidence: ✅ documented · 🟡 consensus · ⚠️ estimate / contested / verify.

---

## 0. Two problems with the brief as stated

1. **Most "new" weapons of the last decade aren't new inventions.** Armed drones date to 2001 (Predator + Hellfire), loitering munitions to the 1980s–90s (IAI Harpy), GPS bomb kits to 1997 (JDAM), and hypersonic concepts to the 1940s ✅. What changed after 2014 is **cost, scale, software, and civilian supply chains**. So the deep section should be organized by **shifts in how war works**, with weapons as evidence. A catalog of new models would date within a year and miss the point.
2. **You can't feel "scale" from a list of dates.** Scale needs a few **fixed measurements tracked across every era**: the same dials, moving. Otherwise the brief past is just a slideshow. See §3.

---

## 1. Five ways to display the chain (pick or combine)

| # | Concept | How it works | Strength | Weakness |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **A** | **Accelerating timeline** | Time is compressed: 2.6 million years take the first ~20% of the scroll, 2014–2026 take ~50% | The layout itself shows acceleration | Needs care so the past doesn't feel rushed |
| **B** | **12 revolutions = 12 rules that broke** | Each old revolution is shown as a belief it destroyed ("Walls keep you safe" broke in 1453) | Short, memorable, about *mentality*, not specs | Needs a strong sentence per revolution |
| **C** | **The six dials** | Same 6 measures in every era: reach, energy, speed of decision, crew, cost, who can own it | Shows **scale** with numbers | Some numbers are estimates and must be badged |
| **D** | **Ancestor pairs** | Every modern weapon is linked to an old ancestor (Shahed ↔ V-1, 1944) | Makes the old part *useful*: it explains the new | Some pairs are analogies, not lineage. Say so |
| **E** | **One battle per revolution** | Each revolution told through the battle where it changed war (Crécy, Constantinople, Somme, Hiroshima, Karabakh…) | Story-driven, human | Battles need context. Longer |

**Recommendation:** **B + C + D on top of A.** The old part is a chain of 12 "broken rules," each with its dials and one battle mentioned in a line. The modern part is 7 deep "shifts," each linked back to its ancestors. That keeps the past short but meaningful, makes the scale visible, and leaves room for the depth you want on the present.

## 3. The six dials: making scale visible

Every revolution card (and every modern chapter) shows the same six measures. Values are **orders of magnitude**, badged.

| Dial | Old end | Middle | Today | Change |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Reach** (strike range) | Bow ~200–300 m 🟡 | V-2 ~320 km (1944) 🟡 | ICBM ~10,000–13,000 km 🟡. A drone over Afghanistan flown from Nevada (2001–) ✅ | ×~50,000 |
| **2. Energy per shot** | Longbow arrow ~100 J 🟡 (order of magnitude) | Musket ball ~2,500 J 🟡; 155 mm shell ~40 MJ of explosive 🟡 | Hiroshima ~63 TJ ✅; Tsar Bomba ~210 PJ ✅ | **×~10¹⁵** (a million billion) |
| **3. Decision speed** (detection → strike) | Days (couriers) ⚠️ | Hours (WWII, 1991 Scud hunt) ⚠️ | Minutes (Ukraine: drone + app + artillery) 🟡 → seconds (autonomous interceptors) ⚠️ | ×~1,000–10,000 |
| **4. People per weapon** | Chariot 2; trireme ~200; ship of the line ~800 🟡 | Bomber 7–11 crew | FPV drone 1–2 operators; a PLA demo of **1 soldier → 200 drones** ⚠️ (claim) | Toward zero |
| **5. Cost** (attacker vs defender) | Knight's kit = a village's yearly output ⚠️ | Tank ~$ millions | FPV ~$400–500 ⚠️ vs a tank; Shahed ~$20–50k ⚠️ vs a ~$4m interceptor ⚠️; interceptor drones claimed at ~$1,000 ⚠️ | **Cheap now beats expensive** (the flip) |
| **6. Who can own it** | Elites (chariot, knight) | States → industrial superpowers (nukes, stealth) | Militias, startups, individuals (commercial drones, cyber) | Democratized |

**Seventh dial (our signature, needs research):** *elements of the periodic table in the weapon.* A bronze sword ≈ 2 main elements (Cu, Sn). A modern guided missile or fighter ≈ dozens ⚠️ (to be counted from bills of materials). That ties scale straight to the site's minerals thesis: **the number of mines behind one weapon exploded.**

## 4. The deep present: 7 shifts (2014–2026)

Each shift = one chapter: **what changed in people's heads · signature weapons & events · the numbers · the value chain behind it · ancestors.**

### Shift 1: Precision became cheap and massive
- **Mentality:** "Precision strike belonged to superpowers" → "every squad has its own air force."
- **Evidence:** TB2 + loitering munitions in Nagorno-Karabakh (2020) ✅; Shahed/Geran (2022–) ✅; FPV mass war in Ukraine (2023–) ✅; Russian **glide-bomb kits** turning Soviet bomb stocks into guided weapons (2023–) 🟡.
- **Numbers:** Ukrainian commanders attribute **70–80% of battlefield casualties** to drones ⚠️ (estimate). A Ukrainian figure says drones caused **96% of Russian casualties in March 2026** ⚠️ (one side's claim). UN: short-range drones caused **27% of civilian casualties in July 2026** ✅ (UN monitoring).
- **Value chain:** NdFeB motor magnets, Li-ion cells, cameras, chips, mostly Chinese → **rare earths, graphite, lithium, cobalt**.
- **Ancestors:** sling & hand grenade (cheap, personal), V-1 (1944) for the Shahed.

### Shift 2: There is no safe rear anymore
- **Mentality:** "Distance from the front = safety" → "a truck in your own country can launch the attack."
- **Evidence:** **Operation Spiderweb (1 Jun 2025):** drones smuggled thousands of km into Russia in trucks hit strategic bombers at several bases. Ukraine claimed 41 aircraft hit. US officials and satellite imagery suggest ~10–21 destroyed ⚠️ (contested). Long-range drone strikes on refineries (2024–) 🟡. Naval drones pushing the Russian Black Sea Fleet back (2024–) ✅. The front-line "kill zone" where nothing can move unseen 🟡.
- **Ancestors:** Trojan horse; WWI no-man's land (defense dominance returns).

### Shift 3: The cost curve flipped
- **Mentality:** "Better and more expensive wins" → "cheap and many can bankrupt the defender."
- **Evidence:** Houthi attacks on Red Sea shipping (2023–) ✅; Iran's missile & drone salvos (2024, June 2025, 2026) ✅; interceptor shortages 🟡. Responses: **interceptor drones** (Ukraine, ~$1,000 claimed ⚠️; OSIRIS UEB-1 unveiled 2026 ⚠️), **lasers** (Iron Beam: reported mostly unused and not yet fully operational in the 2026 Iran war ⚠️), microwaves; the US **Golden Dome** program, with a reported **$452m FY2027 request for directed energy** ⚠️.
- **Ancestors:** WWI fighter planes (built to kill other flyers); Archimedes' "death ray" (legend ⚠️).

### Shift 4: Civilian tech and private companies became the arsenal
- **Mentality:** "Weapons come from defense contractors" → "they come from consumer electronics, startups and one satellite company."
- **Evidence:** Starlink in Ukraine (2022–) ✅; commercial satellite imagery (Maxar, Planet) ✅; battlefield apps and crowdfunding 🟡; 3D printing & garage drone workshops 🟡; new defense-tech firms 🟡.
- **Value chain:** the defense industrial base now runs through **Shenzhen and Taiwan (chips)**, not only arsenals.
- **Ancestors:** railways & telegraph (US Civil War, 1860s): civilian networks became military infrastructure.

### Shift 5: Electrons versus steel
- **Mentality:** "Precision is a fixed advantage" → "precision can be switched off by jamming."
- **Evidence:** Massive GPS jamming & spoofing (Ukraine, Baltic) 🟡; GPS-guided shells and bombs degraded by Russian EW ⚠️; **fiber-optic drones** (2024–) that ignore jamming by going back to a physical wire 🟡; cyber: NotPetya (2017) ✅, Viasat satellite-network hack on 24 Feb 2022 ✅.
- **Ancestors:** wire-guided missiles (1960s–70s), radar countermeasures of WWII ("Window" chaff, 1943).

### Shift 6: The supply chain is the battlefield ★ (the site's climax)
- **Mentality:** "War starts at the border" → "war starts in the mine, the factory and the shipping lane, years before the battle."
- **Evidence:**
  - **Pager & walkie-talkie attack (17–18 Sep 2024):** explosives hidden inside devices before delivery to Hezbollah ✅. A weapon *made of a supply chain*.
  - **China's mineral export controls** (Ga/Ge 2023 → antimony 2024 → rare earths 2025; suspensions expiring **10 & 27 Nov 2026** ⚠️) ✅.
  - **Western chips inside Russian missiles and Shaheds** ✅.
  - **The Western shell shortage** (see 155 mm entry) ✅.
  - **2026 Iran war:** US/Israeli strikes from 28 Feb 2026, Iran's attacks on shipping and the **Strait of Hormuz crisis**, a conditional ceasefire on 8 Apr, a US counter-blockade from 13 Apr, a June MoU that collapsed and expired in Aug, and limited strikes again by Sept ⚠️ (reported, per Wikipedia/CRS summaries: verify). **Research question:** Gulf shipping carries much of the world's oil and LNG. Does the crisis also hit **sulfur, urea/ammonia (the explosives' nitrogen chain), aluminium and helium** ⚠️? If it does, it's the strongest real-time proof of the thesis.
- **Ancestors:** Bronze Age tin routes; the British nitrate blockade of 1914 → Haber-Bosch; the WWII wolfram war.

### Shift 7: Faster than human decision
- **Mentality:** "A human decides who dies" → "humans supervise machines that decide in seconds."
- **Evidence:** hypersonics in combat (Kinzhal 2022, Oreshnik on Dnipro 21 Nov 2024) ✅; AI decision-support for targeting (reported in Gaza 2024 and in the 2026 Iran war, contested ⚠️); autonomous interceptors and terminal guidance on drones 🟡; reported **$13.4 bn** in the US FY2026 request for AI-enabled autonomy ⚠️; PLA swarm demonstrations ⚠️.
- **Ancestors:** the proximity fuze (1943, a machine deciding *when*), the Sidewinder (1956, fire-and-forget).

### Case-study wars woven through the shifts
Nagorno-Karabakh 2020 · Ukraine 2022–2026 · Gaza 2023– · Red Sea 2023– · Israel–Iran June 2025 · the 2026 Iran war. Each chapter cites them; no separate war pages in v1.

## 5. Ancestor pairs (then → now): the bridge between both halves

| Modern | Ancestor | What's the same | What's different |
|---|---|---|---|
| Shahed / Geran | **V-1 flying bomb (1944)** | Slow, cheap, mass, aimed at cities | GPS, satellite links, made with Western chips |
| FPV kamikaze drone | Hand grenade / sling | Cheap, personal, disposable | It *sees* and chooses its target |
| Fiber-optic FPV | Wire-guided anti-tank missile (1960s–70s) | Wire beats jamming | Costs ~1/100th ⚠️ |
| Naval drone | Fire ships (Gravelines 1588), torpedo boats (1880s) | Small and cheap kills big | Remote-controlled via satellite |
| Loitering munition | Landmine | A weapon that waits | It moves and hunts |
| Interceptor drone | WWI fighter plane | Built to kill other flyers | Costs ~$1,000 ⚠️ |
| Glide-bomb kit | Fritz X (1943), JDAM (1997) | Turns dumb bombs smart | Bolted onto Soviet stockpiles |
| Starlink | Telegraph & railway | Civilian network becomes military | Owned by one company |
| Pager attack | Trojan horse | The weapon is hidden inside a gift/purchase | Done at industrial scale |
| Mineral export controls | Tin routes, nitrate blockade, wolfram war | Control the material, control the war | Used *before* any shooting |
